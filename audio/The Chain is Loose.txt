[INTRO — WHISPERED]

THE DEVIL watches.
He does not close the door.
He does not turn the key.

[VERSE 1 — THE CHAIN]

Two figures standing side by side,
Heavy links beneath the light.
Iron shining, black and cold,
Looking stronger than they are.

You call it fate.
You call it need.
You call it something you cannot leave.
But look again—
Look at the ring.

[REFRAIN]

The chain is heavy.
The collar is loose.
The chain is heavy.
The collar is loose.

[VERSE 2 — DESIRE]

Approval.
Comfort.
Money.
Praise.

Habit in the morning,
Laughter in the haze.
Nothing here is evil,
Nothing here commands.

Desire is not a prison
Until you call it who you are.
A tool becomes a shackle
When you forget it is a tool.

[REFRAIN]

THE DEVIL smiles,
but never says,
“You have to stay.”

The chain is heavy.
The collar is loose.
You may want it.
That is not the same as being bound.

[VERSE 3 — THE NAME]

Give the hunger its own name.
Give the habit its own face.
Say, “I want this.”
Say, “I fear this.”
Say, “I choose this anyway.”

Do not call the chain your nature.
Do not call the cage your skin.
What you carry is not always
What you are within.

[BRIDGE — SPOKEN / WHISPERED]

Put your fingers
under the collar.

Slowly.

Feel the space
between the metal
and your throat.

Is it tight?

Is it locked?

Did anyone
ever say
you could not remove it?

[VERSE 4 — THE CHOICE]

THE DEVIL does not disappear.
The wine is still sweet.
The room is still warm.
The applause still sounds beautiful.

The habit still waits.
The comfort still calls.
The gold still shines.
The joke still makes you laugh.

And none of that
is the problem.

The question is simpler.

Do you wear the chain
because you must—

or because
you have not tried
to take it off?

[FINAL REFRAIN]

The chain is heavy.
The collar is loose.

The chain is heavy.
The collar is loose.

No broken lock.
No shattered door.
No battle won.
No demon slain.

Only your hand
beneath the ring.

Only the space
you never felt before.

[OUTRO — NEAR SILENCE]

THE DEVIL watches.

You touch the collar.

It moves.

You could remove it.

...

Why are you still wearing it?
