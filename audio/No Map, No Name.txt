[INTRO]

THE FOOL stands
where the ground gives way.

A white rose.
A small dog.
A sky with no instructions.

[VERSE 1]

At the edge of the cliff,
with a light little pack,
I carry less
than I thought I would need.

No grand design.
No careful speech.
Only the wind
and the next small reach.

[REFRAIN]

No map, no name,
no promise to explain.
The road does not need
to be finished to begin.

No map, no name,
no reason to be sure.
You do not need the whole road
to take one step.

[VERSE 2]

The dog at my feet
trusts the morning first.
The rose in my hand
does not ask where.

Nothing is settled.
Nothing is closed.
The world is still
too wide for categories.

I have not learned
enough to be afraid
of every possible fall.

[REFRAIN]

No map, no name,
no promise to explain.
The road does not need
to be finished to begin.

No map, no name,
no reason to be sure.
You do not need the whole road
to take one step.

[BRIDGE]

Call it foolish.
Call it faith.
Call it the moment
before a life has language.

The bag is small
because I have not yet
filled it with old conclusions.

What waits ahead
does not need my certainty.
Only my arrival.

[VERSE 3]

THE FOOL looks forward,
not backward.
Not at the drop,
but at the opening.

Today is not asking
for mastery.
Not perfect timing.
Not complete preparation.

Only this—

place your foot
where the air begins,
and let the world
meet you there.

[FINAL REFRAIN]

No map, no name,
and still I go.
No final answer,
and still I know—

you do not need the whole road
to take one step.

No map, no name,
light heart, open hand.
Sometimes courage
is only this:

begin.

[OUTRO]

THE FOOL moves.
The dog follows.
The rose stays bright.

And the empty space ahead
becomes
the road.
