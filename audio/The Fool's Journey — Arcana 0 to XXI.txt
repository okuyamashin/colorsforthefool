[0 — THE FOOL]

THE FOOL walks out beneath the sky,
A white rose dancing in the air.
The road is nameless, wide and bright,
From zero, I begin somewhere.

[I — THE MAGICIAN]

THE MAGICIAN raises one hand high,
One hand toward the earth below.
Sword and cup, the wand, the coin—
The first true spell is: I will go.

[II — THE HIGH PRIESTESS]

THE HIGH PRIESTESS waits between
The black and white, the hidden door.
She holds the moon inside her silence;
Listen, and you may hear more.

[III — THE EMPRESS]

THE EMPRESS walks through fields of grain,
The river rises, forests bloom.
Love gives shape to living things,
And makes the empty world a room.

[IV — THE EMPEROR]

THE EMPEROR sits upon the stone,
In scarlet robes, his borders drawn.
To guard a world, he must stand firm—
Order holds until the dawn.

[V — THE HIEROPHANT]

THE HIEROPHANT hears the ancient bell,
Old words pass from hand to hand.
Those who walked the road before us
Leave their wisdom where we stand.

[VI — THE LOVERS]

THE LOVERS stand where two roads part,
Two open eyes, one choice to make.
I choose you, and I choose myself;
Love begins with what we take.

[VII — THE CHARIOT]

THE CHARIOT leaves the city gates,
Black and white move side by side.
Fear and doubt become the reins—
Forward, forward, now we ride.

[VIII — STRENGTH]

STRENGTH stands softly by the lion,
Gentle hands and quiet eyes.
Not every beast is won by force;
Kindness too can conquer cries.

[IX — THE HERMIT]

THE HERMIT climbs the lonely height,
One small lantern in the snow.
It cannot light the whole wide world,
But shows the next step where to go.

[X — WHEEL OF FORTUNE]

WHEEL OF FORTUNE turns and turns,
Kings rise high and travelers fall.
No hand can hold the moving future;
Round and round, it carries all.

[XI — JUSTICE]

JUSTICE holds the sword upright,
The scales are still, the balance clear.
Every choice becomes an answer;
Every road has brought me here.

[XII — THE HANGED MAN]

THE HANGED MAN sees the world reversed,
Suspended where the hours slow.
When there is nowhere left to travel,
A different truth begins to show.

[XIII — DEATH]

DEATH rides quietly on a pale white horse,
The season ends, the old leaves fall.
Nothing vanishes forever—
It changes shape, and that is all.

[XIV — TEMPERANCE]

TEMPERANCE pours from cup to cup,
Two streams becoming something new.
Neither burning, neither frozen—
Harmony is passing through.

[XV — THE DEVIL]

THE DEVIL smiles: “What do you want?
Everything you crave is near.”
The chains are loose, the lock is open—
Still, my own desire keeps me here.

[XVI — THE TOWER]

THE TOWER shatters under lightning,
The crown comes falling through the flame.
Walls we thought would stand forever
Break—and truth survives the same.

[XVII — THE STAR]

THE STAR shines over quiet water,
Hope pours softly on the land.
Even after loss and darkness,
There is still a light at hand.

[XVIII — THE MOON]

THE MOON draws paths across the night,
Dog and wolf cry to the sky.
Dream and fear move on the water;
Not all shadows tell a lie.

[XIX — THE SUN]

THE SUN rises, warm and golden,
A child rides beneath its flame.
Every hidden shadow loosens;
To be alive is joy enough to name.

[XX — JUDGEMENT]

JUDGEMENT sounds the waking trumpet,
Sleeping voices lift their eyes.
Not to punish what has happened—
But to call the soul to rise.

[XXI — THE WORLD]

THE WORLD turns whole within the circle,
Four bright guardians watch outside.
Every road becomes one story,
Every step is gathered inside.

The end becomes another opening,
The circle closes, then begins—
And somewhere at the edge of morning,
THE FOOL steps out again.
