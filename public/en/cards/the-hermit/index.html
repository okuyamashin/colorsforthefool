THE HERMIT

隠者 · IX

Upright

The Hermit stands alone on a quiet mountain, lantern raised just enough to show his next steps. He is not running from the world, only choosing a pause from its noise to find personal clarity. His wisdom comes from walking a path that his own light reveals, accepting that not every answer lies far away.

Solitude here is gentle, a space to listen for what is usually drowned out. This is not isolation as punishment, but a mindful choice to walk slowly and observe. The Hermit teaches that insight is often found near, not in distant promises.

Receiving this card suggests trusting your own small discoveries instead of seeking endless advice. Let your own light show you where to go next, and allow progress to come from patience and presence on your own journey.

Reversed

The Hermit’s lantern now shines only inward, failing to light the path ahead. Solitude becomes a hiding place, not a source of insight, and the mountain feels more like a refuge than a vantage point. The urge to withdraw grows, even when the time to rejoin others has come.

When distance becomes the goal, all outside voices are shut out and labeled as noise. Reflection turns into avoidance, and wisdom is lost in the echo chamber of isolation. The lantern’s glow is wasted, no longer guiding anyone—including yourself.

This card advises you to turn your attention outward, letting your light reach beyond your own space. Consider who else might benefit from your insight, and whether your retreat is still serving you. It may now be time to come down and reconnect.
