THE HIGH PRIESTESS

II

Upright

A woman sits quietly between two pillars, her scroll only half-unfolded on her lap. She does not reveal all she knows, preferring to let her knowledge linger in silence. Answers are not rushed; this card suggests there is wisdom in waiting and observing before speaking or acting.

Beneath the visible knowledge, another book is hidden—intuition, dreams, subtle feelings that do not yet fit into words. These are not unformed thoughts, but truths that have not found their moment. The High Priestess honors the mysteries that dwell just beyond explanation.

Today, this card invites you to sit with uncertainty, to let your questions remain open for now. Wait between the pillars and trust that understanding will come in its own time. There is value in patience and in not forcing answers to appear.

Reversed

The scroll is either unopened, its secrets withheld, or thrown wide, its contents scattered and misunderstood. Silence becomes a barrier, and intuition can harden into unfounded certainty. The High Priestess reversed warns of mistaking vague discomforts for conclusions.

With this card upside down, the quiet inner voice is drowned out or replaced by outside noise. You might ignore what you should hear or reveal what should remain hidden. The seat between the pillars is left empty, and the sense of waiting is lost.

Today, this card asks you not to force answers from what is still unclear. Let your questions rest a while longer; it is not yet time to decide. Sometimes, wisdom lies in waiting and letting the scroll remain closed another night.
