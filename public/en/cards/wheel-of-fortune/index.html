WHEEL OF FORTUNE

運命の輪 · X

Upright

The Wheel of Fortune turns, exchanging positions between those who ascend and those who descend. This card reminds us that fate spins independently of our personal efforts, reorganizing the world beyond our control. It is not simply a symbol of luck, but of the inevitable shifts and cycles that life brings.

True fortune is not found solely in rising or gaining. The nature of the wheel is to keep moving, ensuring that no position is permanent. This card offers a quiet caution against mistaking your current situation—whether comfort or struggle—for a fixed and unchanging seat.

Today, the card suggests paying attention to your own place on the turning wheel, rather than trying to grasp at or force the changes themselves. It invites you to observe the motion of life, recognizing that your role and circumstances are part of an ongoing cycle, not a final destination.

Reversed

It can seem as though the wheel is spinning in place, giving the illusion of movement without progress. There may be a longing to return to a prior state, even as change is already happening. Sometimes, it is easy to label only the difficult turns as 'fate,' while disconnecting from your own role in the experience.

When reversed, the Wheel of Fortune can make repetition feel like a curse. The same events may not be repeating, but rather, the way you respond is keeping the cycle stuck. The wheel’s pause is not about external circumstances, but about the inner resistance to letting things move forward.

Today, this card asks you to acknowledge the spin you are resisting. Ceding your place to the flow, even briefly, allows the natural cycle of change to resume. Accepting the motion—however uncertain—breaks the spell of stasis and opens the way for genuine transformation.
