THE MOON

月 · XVIII

Upright

Under the uncertain glow of the moon, only part of the path is visible between two silent towers. The world loses its sharpness; details dissolve into shadows, and only what is close at hand can be trusted. The dog barks for the known and comforting, while the wolf stirs with the wild and unnamed within you.

Boundaries set by habit or reason are present, but the journey asks you to move forward despite incomplete information. The Moon card encourages trust in feelings and instincts, even when facts are hidden. Progress is not about waiting for full understanding, but about taking the next visible step.

This card calls for gentle courage—walk the path you can see, acknowledge your uncertainty, and accept that some mysteries are part of the journey. Let your intuition guide you through the haze, rather than demanding the world reveal everything at once.

Reversed

The path disappears beneath fog, or else every shadow feels threatening, shaped by anxious imagination. When The Moon is reversed, uncertainty grows into confusion. The unknown becomes a stage for fears to play out, and the mind fills gaps with worry rather than patience.

Instincts may be misread or ignored, leading you away from safe ground or into self-deception. You might try to force clarity with quick decisions or false certainty, painting over ambiguity with answers that do not fit. The night feels overwhelming, and uneasy feelings cloud judgment.

This card reminds you to resist letting anxiety take control. Trust only what you can see right now and avoid letting fear invent stories about the future. Move carefully, step by step, without demanding to know more than what is honestly revealed.
