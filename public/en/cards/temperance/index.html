TEMPERANCE

XIV

Upright

Temperance invites you to blend opposing elements with care, like water flowing gently between two cups. It is not about denying one side or clinging to the other, but about gradual exchange. Through patient movement between differences—like urgency and patience, warmth and coolness—a new, balanced state emerges that neither held alone.

The card suggests that wisdom comes from repeated, mindful integration, not from quick solutions or rigid choices. Each time you move between alternatives, you refine the mixture and discover a unique harmony. The process is gentle but deliberate, building something new from what was once divided.

Temperance calls for quiet attention to the spaces between extremes. Instead of seeking one right answer, allow yourself to revisit both sides, letting them inform and transform each other. True balance is found in the subtle, ongoing act of blending, not in erasing difference.

Reversed

Temperance reversed shows water spilling outside the cups, signaling imbalance or a failed attempt to combine. One flavor may overpower while the other is ignored, or you may hesitate so much that nothing is truly mixed. The idea of balance becomes a shield to avoid real decisions, leaving things incomplete.

This card warns against half-hearted blending or favoring one side while hiding the other. Stagnation can result when integration is neglected, and the richness of true combination is lost. Indecision and neglect make the mixture thin and unsatisfying.

Look closely at what is spilling or missing. Temperance reversed asks for smaller, more precise steps and honest attention to what has been set aside. Restore the balance by adjusting your approach, taking care to include what was forgotten, and letting the process become whole again.
