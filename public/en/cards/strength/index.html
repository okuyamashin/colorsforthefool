Today's lucky color, drawn from the tarot

Draw a card

STRENGTH

VIII

Upright

Strength is not about how tightly you hold or how much you can suppress. Instead, it is shown by how gently you can rest your hand beside the wildness in yourself and the world. The lion stands both for outside obstacles and for your own passionate energy; it is not meant to be crushed, but understood and acknowledged.

If you kill this fire, you lose your courage along with it. Yet, if you allow your touch to stay soft, you let your heat become a living rhythm rather than a force of destruction. Strength is the practice of remaining close and open, not retreating or forcing, but holding presence with tenderness.

Today, this card encourages you to stop gripping so hard. True strength is shown in the way you remain connected, not by how much you can overpower. Let your gentleness be the proof of your courage.

  • Gold #FFD700 Carry patience and gentle power into each moment.
  • GoldenRod #DAA520 Strength is quiet, persistence is gentle.
  • Orange #FFA500 Let patience and quiet action carry you through.
  • PeachPuff #FFDAB9 Strength unfolds with softness, every action a quiet offering.
  • Moccasin #FFE4B5 Let quiet actions speak.
  • Linen #FAF0E6 Silence is a form of strength.
  • ForestGreen #228B22 Move quietly; your roots run deep.
  • Pink #FFC0CB Move with gentle resolve, and let quiet acts speak for themselves.
  • LemonChiffon #FFFACD Gentle persistence holds more power than a shout.
  • Wheat #F5DEB3 Let strength be the quiet that endures.

Reversed

When reversed, Strength reveals a hand gone rigid, fingers clenched not out of mastery but out of fear. Though it may look as if you have tamed the lion, the grip comes from anxiety, not confidence. Sometimes, it is a gentle facade masking doubt in your own passion, causing you to look away rather than meet your inner beast.

Here, endurance becomes a substitute for true strength. There is a difference between simply holding on and actually building trust with what is wild within you. The effort to control can turn into mere repression, leaving you disconnected from your own vitality.

This card now suggests loosening your grasp, even just a little. Notice what remains pulsing beneath your palm when you ease up. The resilient rhythm you feel is your real strength for today.

  • Crimson #DC143C Let intent be tempered by softness, and find strength in yielding.
  • Red #FF0000 There is courage in loosening the hand.
  • DarkRed #8B0000 Strength sometimes means knowing when to loosen your grip.
  • FireBrick #B22222 Let gentleness guide your hand where force would only harden the day.
  • Sienna #A0522D Patience and persistence grow deepest roots in yielding ground.
  • DarkGoldenRod #B8860B May the day's force be softened by patience.
  • Brown #A52A2A Endurance comes from yielding as much as from strength.
  • SaddleBrown #8B4513 Patient hands mend what force has strained.
  • DimGray #696969 Patience under pressure; even stone yields, in time.
  • Black #000000 Let restraint meet persistence; let the hush make room for wisdom.
