THE WORLD

XXI

Upright

A figure dances gracefully within a ring, watched over from all corners. This card signifies the culmination of a journey, a cycle that has fully borne fruit. Completion is not an empty state, but one where all experiences and lessons are visible together, woven into a single, harmonious whole.

Standing at the end, you find that closure opens the way to new beginnings. What has finished now becomes the threshold to something unknown, inviting you forward like the Fool stepping into the next adventure. The boundaries between past and future blur, and the world feels both complete and filled with possibility.

Today, this card reminds you to focus on what has come full circle. Instead of worrying about what is missing or unfinished, take a moment to acknowledge and celebrate the entirety of your journey so far. Honor the wholeness that exists, and let it be a foundation for what comes next.

  • ForestGreen #228B22 Let the spirit of the deep woods guide you onward.
  • Olive #808000 Completion is within reach; the circle ties itself.
  • Gold #FFD700 May your day end with a ribbon tied and a promise kept.
  • Purple #800080 The World completes the dance with grace.
  • Violet #EE82EE The garland closes, the ribbon is tied, and the journey finds its rest.
  • Red #FF0000 A ribbon is tied; the circle completes.
  • White #FFFFFF Move quietly, and let the world gather itself around you.
  • Wheat #F5DEB3 May your work nourish, and your circuit be whole.
  • Cornsilk #FFF8DC A soft ending, a gentle beginning, the world’s circuit gently tied.
  • SkyBlue #87CEEB A day to breathe deeply and let the world feel complete.

Reversed

The dance pauses just before the ring is closed. There is hesitation to take the final step needed for completion, perhaps out of fear that what you have achieved is not perfect or sufficient. Sometimes, you may find yourself circling the same path, unable to close one chapter and move on.

When the World is reversed, fulfillment always seems to shift just beyond your reach—each accomplishment leading to another set of requirements or expectations. Satisfaction is postponed, and the sense of closure remains elusive, leaving you feeling stuck in a cycle of striving without arrival.

Today, this card invites you to recognize a cycle you can close, even if it is not perfect. Before adding to your list of unfinished tasks, allow yourself to complete something, however small. By choosing resolution over perpetual waiting, you create space for new journeys to begin.

  • OliveDrab #6B8E23 A half-tied wreath, a harvest left waiting, the world not quite closed.
  • DarkOliveGreen #556B2F There is wisdom in pausing just before the knot is tied.
  • DarkKhaki #BDB76B Let the unfinished find comfort in what still endures.
  • Khaki #F0E68C Carry a thread or a scrap as you wait for the circle to close.
  • Tan #D2B48C The day waits at the threshold; the story continues.
  • Sienna #A0522D The world’s arc is not quite closed today; leave your own circle open.
  • Brown #A52A2A Patience is the gift of the unfinished circle.
  • Gray #808080 Completion can wait; linger softly on the edge.
  • DimGray #696969 Pause, and wait alongside the world.
  • SlateGray #708090 The knot is not yet tied, but the stone holds its place.
