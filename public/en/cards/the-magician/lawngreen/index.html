Draw a card / THE MAGICIAN

LawnGreen

魔術師 · ローングリーン

Upright · #7CFC00

On a table in the heart of a tended garden, the Magician gathers four tools: a wand cut from willow, a cup of fired clay, a sharp-edged knife, and a sturdy coin worn smooth by use. These are not distant or mystical but real, weathered by hands and seasons, as familiar as the garden path underfoot. The Magician’s will is not a force apart from the world but something woven into each act: lifting the wand to summon a breeze, pouring water from the cup to nourish beds, drawing the knife along the stems of herbs, pressing the coin’s weight into the palm as payment for seeds.

Beyond the table, the carefully kept lawn stretches out in quiet invitation. Short, springy turf forms a welcome carpet, shaped by careful labor. Early morning finds the groundskeeper at work, rolling the earth to keep it even, guiding the old push mower in straight lines, and pausing now and then to clear away dandelions before they scatter. The air carries the scent of clipped growth, cool and alive. On weekends, this close-clipped field becomes a ground for games: wooden balls tapping through hoops, shoes left behind at the edge so feet can press directly into the earth’s living mat. Laughter skims across the open space as children tumble and neighbors talk, their presence part of the cycle that keeps the grass thriving.

In old towns and bustling cities alike, the common’s great expanse gathers people and stories. Here sheep have grazed, keeping the turf close and lush, and village fairs have sprung up beneath banners on breezy days. Cricket matches, picnics spread on woven blankets, and quiet afternoon walks all leave their gentle mark. The land is cared for by many small acts: soil forked and aired, worn spots mended after rain, seeds pressed in by thumb and knuckle. Even in winter, frost etches delicate patterns across the blades, softened by the promise of return. The strong, steady surface beneath is living testament to care shared across generations.

The Magician’s wisdom lies in marrying will with the everyday: wielding the wand like a gardener tending young shoots, the cup like one who draws water from a stone well. The knife serves not only for cutting but for harvesting chives and mint for midday bread, the coin finds its purpose at the market’s edge, exchanging hands with warm intent. Above and below are joined in the work: rain falls to feed the roots, sun draws the lawn upright, each daily cycle restoring what was spent. Every tool at the table is a memory, an invitation to shape the world with intention.

Today, walk barefoot outside at dawn and feel the living carpet under your feet. Gather a handful of fresh herbs to crush and breathe in, letting the scent remind you of growth. Give a coin freely in kindness, or sit with your well-worn tools and consider how best to use them for something new.
