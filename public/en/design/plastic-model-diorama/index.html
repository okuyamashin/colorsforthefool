Plastic model diorama

On the worktable sits a temple smaller than a street. The horns are brass gears. The eyes are red lamps. The chain was glued on after the model was finished. Brushes and bottles of paint remain outside the card. Only the last number in the instructions was never printed. Close the lid because it is finished, and the chain on the table stops at the measure of your wrist.
