Old classical tarot

The edge of the paper is worn, and the ink has bled into the fibers. Horns come two by two. The torch is in the right hand. The upturned palm is in the left. The chain passes through the rings of the pedestal. The two naked figures stand at the same distance. A flaw in the plate remains in the same place no matter how many copies are printed. The lines are plainly an old picture. Only the inner width of the ring is printed to the measure of your throat.
