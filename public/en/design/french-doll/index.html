French dolls

A fine crack runs across a porcelain cheek. The crack has the shape of a smile that already happened. Horns come out between the curls. A lace collar is where the chain begins. The candle flames stand at the same height on the left and the right. Each eyelash was set in by hand, and stops just before a blink. She is lovely, so you lift her. At the joint, the doll bends to the angle of your arm.
