Cubism

One face turns toward more than one hour at once. Horns break along the edge of a plane. The chain crosses at right angles between cubes. There is little color, and every plane argues with the next. The eye you took for the front looks sideways from inside a neighboring triangle. Try to decide which side is the front, and the remaining planes begin to assemble your profile from outside the card.
