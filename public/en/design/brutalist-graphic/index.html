Brutalist graphic

Heavy type blocks the bottom of the card like a wall. Masses of red and black become stairs. Figures are made of planes. Horns stand on rectangular blocks. Even the scuffs in the paper belong to the plan. Count the steps and the way up never matches the way down. Drop your eyes to read the type, and the letters are heavier than the chain. They stop your gaze halfway up the stairs.
