Cyber mysticism

Red circuits run straight down between columns of glass. The horns are metal. The ring on the chest beats. The chain carries light to the throat. The wet floor draws every sign once more, upside down. The inverted letters have one stroke more than the upright ones. Step closer to read them, and the reflection fastens your face to the chain first.
