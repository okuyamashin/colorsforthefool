Watercolor

Before the water dries, color escapes past the outline. The red of the wings mixes into the blue of the night. The torch fire becomes a drop and runs downward. Only the links of the chain remain, gold that does not bleed. The water stops at the edge of the paper. Only the outside of that edge stays dry. Press the edge you thought was dry, and one drop still wet spreads in the shape of your wrist.
