Ancient Egypt

Every eye looks this way while the faces stay in profile. A sun disk sits between the horns. Crook and flail occupy the two hands. The blank is filled with hieroglyphs. A black dog does not move on the pedestal. The chain descends from the collar to the knee. The writing can be read from the left or from the right, and either way it arrives at the same name. Follow the signs with a finger, and the next one has the shape of your name.
