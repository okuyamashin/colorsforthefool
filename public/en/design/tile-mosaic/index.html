Tile mosaic

Small stones make the skin before they make an outline. Horns bend through shard after shard. The trident never becomes one line, and still it stands. Squint and it is a picture. Come close and it is only stone. The stones of the eyes are set one size smaller than the rest. Press one stone, and the stone beside it is already missing, in the shape of your fingerprint.
