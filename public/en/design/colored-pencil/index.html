Colored pencil

Along the tooth of the paper, short strokes make skin by the tens of thousands. The grooves of the horns, the folds of the red cloth, the gold of the chain: all of them are the track of the same lead. A star on the floor draws a circle. The two figures bow their faces. Pencil dust remains, thinly, just outside the outline, in the shape of a finger. Trace the direction of a stroke, and the lead moves onto the paper on your side and starts the outline of a throat at the same pressure.
