Mezzotint

A dark ground is scraped back into light afterward. Only the edges of the horns, the links of the chain, and the lifted throat are white. Most of the wings remain on the side of night. The star above the head is faint. The city is past the fog. The dust that was scraped away has fallen nowhere on the card. While you look at the places made bright, the dark that was left unscraped is sitting in your shape.
