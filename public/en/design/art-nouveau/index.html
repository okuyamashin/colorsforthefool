Art Nouveau

The vines do not cross the frame. They are the shape of the frame. Grapes and lilies draw the same curve on the left and the right. Horns continue the hair. The chain hangs like a necklace. Even the fire bends to the line of the smoke. No vine has a root. Each one begins partway along the frame. Follow a curve with your eyes, and the vine takes one extra turn around your wrist.
